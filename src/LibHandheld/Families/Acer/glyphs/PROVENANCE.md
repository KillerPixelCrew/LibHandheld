# Acer glyph provenance

Standard gamepad glyphs reuse the library's existing PromptFont outlines from the Generic family,
with their original SIL OFL attribution in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. Their source revision and font
digest remain unchanged. The Atlas profile maps Task, Predator, M1 and M2 to the new native OEM IDs.

These are stock semantic glyphs, not Acer logos or exact illustrations of its physical buttons. No
Predator Sense artwork is redistributed. Physical button names and placement follow the reported
Atlas 8 input inventory; hardware acceptance remains outstanding.
