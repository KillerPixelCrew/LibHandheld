# Third-party notices

This folder holds the ATKACPI access that the ROG Ally and generic ASUS families share. It carries
no glyph artwork, fonts or other third-party assets; each family that draws ASUS glyphs lists them
in its own notices. The ACPI device IDs follow HC 1.3.1.6 as behavioural reference; no HC
application source is included. The evidence for these controls is recorded in the ROG Ally
`PROVENANCE.md`.
